STEP 1 · WORD LIST

Ten words you need before the briefing

Read each one: its meaning, and the morphemes — the prefix, root and suffix — that build that meaning. Almost every word in this topic is assembled from a small Greek and Latin kit, and the word builders in Step 3 use near-miss decoy morphemes. One idea keeps everything straight: gender identity and attraction are two different axes — trans/cis answer one question, homo/hetero/bi/pan/a answer the other.
